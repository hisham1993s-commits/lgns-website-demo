Our story

One kitchen. Every craving.

London Grill N Shake is a multi-cuisine restaurant chain with 23 years in the industry: Mayiladuthurai since 2001, London since 2009, Navalur-Chennai since 2014, The Farmer in Padur-Chennai since 2018, and Kumbakonam since 2021.

Guests come for traditional Chettinad, Thai, Chinese, Japanese and more, cooked by our special chef's team. Many bowls, many tastes.
